**Durability Alarm** is a client-side mod for **Minecraft 26.3 / Fabric** that warns you when your items are running low on durability.

Keep track of tools, weapons, armor, elytra and shields while you play. The mod monitors your main inventory, hotbar, equipment and both hands.

- **On-screen and sound alerts:** orange warnings at 10% remaining durability and red warnings at 3% by default. Both thresholds are configurable.
- **Detailed HUD:** item icon, name, exact remaining durability, percentage and inventory location.
- **Multiple items:** additional pages cycle automatically when several items need attention.
- **Settings on K:** choose the panel position, alert volume, reminder interval and inventory scope. The key can be rebound.
- **Modded item support** for items using Minecraft's standard durability system.
- **English and Russian interface.**

Install on the client. Requires **Fabric Loader 0.19.5+**, **Fabric API for 26.3 (0.161.0+26.3 or later)** and **Java 25+**.

Items inside chests, shulker boxes and bundles, and custom accessory slots are not monitored.
